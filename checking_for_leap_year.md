# Checking For Leap Year
## Problem
Checking for leap year is simple problem in which we have to find either the number that we have entered is a leap year or not.
For context _leap-year_ is a year that comes after every 4 years.
## Solution
For to solve this problem I have firstly made a variable called `year`. This will store the input that the user will enter.
And than I have used the concept of two way selection method of c++. In which te program will work in the matched condition. And the condition that will work for leap year is that if that year divided by 4 gives the reminder of 0 then that is a  `leap-year`.
